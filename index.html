ℹ️ Accounting Accrual Convention: ON_INSTALLMENT_DUE_DATE ▼
Under the authoritative ON_INSTALLMENT_DUE_DATE convention executed in PostgreSQL, contractual interest is recognized as earned (Accrued Interest) strictly when an installment's due date arrives (due_date ≤ today). Future scheduled installments (due_date > today) do not accrue unearned interest prematurely.
