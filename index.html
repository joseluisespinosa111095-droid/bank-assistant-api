Calculation Method & Calendar Assumption Disclosure:

• Flat Interest on Original Principal: Monthly interest is calculated strictly on the original principal (Original Principal × Monthly Rate) and does not decrease as principal is amortized.
• Weeks vs. Calendar Months Notice: 16 calendar weeks (112 days) do not equal 4 exact calendar months. This scenario models 4 full contractual monthly interest periods distributed evenly across 16 weekly installments (with exact cent-rounding adjustment on the final installment if required). Contractual prorating for partial months and early payoff rules require explicit business rule approval before loan origination.
